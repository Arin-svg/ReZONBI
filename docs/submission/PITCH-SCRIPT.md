# Re:ZONBI pitch and demo

Working three-minute script; adjust to the organizer's actual time limit. Add the entrant/team name before presenting. Proposed audience and positioning below are design choices, not market-research findings.

## 0:00–0:25 — Concept

“Re:ZONBI is a mobile survival roguelite set in the infected streets of New Haven. You play as Rin, fighting to survive an escalating horde. Automatic shooting makes the game approachable on a touchscreen, while movement, area attacks, dodging, and upgrade choices keep the player involved.”

## 0:25–0:50 — Audience and distinction

“Our intended audience is players who enjoy action survival games and repeatable mobile sessions. Re:ZONBI combines a neon urban setting with gunfire that attracts more infected, six upgrade families, and recurring Tyrant encounters. Saving and continuing supports interrupted play.”

## 0:50–1:50 — Demonstrate the build

Show START and the survival objective. Move with the joystick, let the pistol acquire a target, then demonstrate slash and dodge. Show a level-up choice if available. Explain the noise pressure and the next boss milestone. Use a clearly identified recording from the actual build if a live boss encounter cannot fit within the pitch; do not promise it will appear in the first minute.

Show pause, English/Malay settings, and return-home/continue. Briefly show STATS. Keep a local gameplay recording ready as a presentation backup; verify that it matches the submitted version.

## 1:50–2:15 — Story and value

“Rin's immediate story is survival in New Haven. The playable experience asks the player to persevere, adapt, and make decisions under pressure. This submission focuses on that survival loop.”

Do not claim a rescue mission, complete campaign, educational outcome, or story ending that is not implemented. The resilience theme is an interpretation of the experience, not a measured learning result.

## 2:15–2:45 — Ownership and implementation

Explain your actual contribution to design, Cocos Creator implementation, integration, tuning, and testing. Show the six upgrade families, enemy behaviors, procedural city, and save system as concrete implementation examples. Disclose AI-assisted code/art and third-party assets accurately using CREDITS.md; do not claim every asset was drawn or composed by the entrant.

## 2:45–3:00 — Close

“The submission is a Huawei Quick Game RPK. HMS services are not integrated in this version. Re:ZONBI focuses on a repeatable survival experience with accessible controls, escalating combat, and choices that change each run.”
