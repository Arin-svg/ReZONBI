# Re:ZONBI mobile user guide

Re:ZONBI is a mobile survival game. Play as Rin and survive the infected streets of New Haven for as long as possible. Use the phone's touchscreen to move, attack, and choose upgrades.

## Open the game on your phone

The submitted version is a Huawei Quick Game RPK. Open the supplied package using a compatible Huawei Quick Game test environment on your phone. An RPK is a Quick Game package, not an Android APK.

1. Hold your phone sideways in landscape orientation.
2. Open Re:ZONBI and tap **START** on the home menu to begin a new run. If you already have a saved run, tap **CONTINUE** to resume it instead.
3. Read Rin's opening dialogue. Tap **NEXT** to advance, then **CLOSE** to begin playing.
4. Hold the phone with both hands. Use your left thumb for movement and your right thumb for the action buttons.

Button names in this guide use the English setting. You can select English or Malay in **SETTINGS**.

## Touch controls

| Action | How to use it on your phone |
| --- | --- |
| Move | Touch and hold the joystick at the bottom left. Drag your thumb in the direction you want Rin to move. Lift your thumb to stop. |
| Shoot | Rin fires automatically when an enemy is visible and within range. Move into position; you do not need to tap a fire button. |
| Area slash | Tap the blade icon near the bottom right, to the left of dodge. It attacks nearby enemies and can hit several at once. |
| Dodge | Tap the dodge icon at the bottom right to evade. Use it while moving to escape danger. |
| Pause | Tap the pause icon at the top right. Tap **RESUME** in the pause menu to return to play. |
| Select a menu option or upgrade | Tap the button or upgrade you want on the screen. |

You can keep your left thumb on the joystick while tapping slash or dodge with your right thumb. After using an ability, wait for its cooldown before using it again. The base cooldown is 15 seconds for slash and 3 seconds for dodge; upgrades can change combat behavior.

## Survive and upgrade

Watch Rin's **HP** at the top left. Enemy attacks reduce health; the run ends when Rin falls. Keep moving, leave yourself an escape route, and avoid being surrounded. Gunfire attracts infected, so stay alert even while shooting automatically.

Collect **EXP** drops from defeated enemies to fill the experience bar. When you level up, combat pauses and upgrade choices appear. Tap an offered upgrade to select it and continue playing. The six upgrade families are Bullet Storm, Blade Cyclone, Shadow Dash, Electric Pulse, Bloodlust, and Zombie Magnet.

The Tyrant becomes eligible after each 100 ordinary-zombie kills. Watch for its attack warnings and move out of danger. After defeating it, tap **NEXT STAGE** to continue survival. Defeating a Tyrant does not end a story campaign.

If Rin falls, tap **RESTART ENCOUNTER** to start again or **RETURN TO HOMEPAGE** to return to the home menu.

## Pause and change settings

Tap the pause icon at the top right to open these options:

- **RESUME**: continue the current run.
- **SETTINGS**: adjust music and sound-effect volume or mute audio, choose graphics quality, and switch between English and Malay. Low graphics disables scene shadows.
- **TUTORIAL**: review the gameplay instructions.
- **RETURN TO HOMEPAGE**: return to the home menu. Confirm the prompt to save your checkpoint and leave the run.

## Save and continue later

Before taking a break, tap pause, then **RETURN TO HOMEPAGE**, and confirm the save prompt. When you open the game again, tap **CONTINUE** to restore the saved checkpoint.

The game also saves every 15 seconds during active play and when pausing. Continuing restores the latest saved checkpoint, so it may not match the exact moment you closed the game. Saving requires available local storage on your phone.

Confirming a new run replaces the previous saved checkpoint. Death clears that checkpoint. Clearing the game's app data removes local saves and records.

Tap **STATS** on the home menu to view your personal best kill count, best survival duration, and current saved run. The two personal bests are tracked separately and may come from different runs. They remain after death or restarting.
