# Re:ZONBI user guide

Submission target: Huawei Quick Game RPK. Source-reviewed on 30 October 2026; final-device verification is pending. An RPK is a Quick Game package, not an Android APK. Use the compatible Huawei Quick Game test environment to load the submitted package.

## Start and objective

Open Re:ZONBI in landscape. Choose START and advance Rin's dialogue with NEXT, then CLOSE. Survive the infected streets of New Haven for as long as possible. Defeating a Tyrant lets you continue; it does not finish a story campaign.

## Controls

| Action | Phone | Desktop preview |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Drag the left joystick | WASD or arrow keys |
| Shoot | Automatic when a nearby enemy is visible and in range | Automatic |
| Area slash | Tap the sword control | K |
| Dodge | Tap dodge | Space |
| Pause | Tap pause | Escape |

Gunfire attracts infected. Keep moving, avoid being surrounded, and use slash and dodge when available. Base slash cooldown is 15 seconds; base dodge cooldown is 3 seconds. Upgrades can change combat behavior.

Collect EXP drops to level up, then choose one of the offered upgrades. Combat pauses while choosing. The six upgrade families are Bullet Storm, Blade Cyclone, Shadow Dash, Electric Pulse, Bloodlust, and Zombie Magnet.

The Tyrant becomes eligible after each 100 ordinary-zombie kills. Avoid its telegraphed attacks. After defeating it, choose NEXT STAGE to resume survival.

## Pause, settings, and saving

The pause menu offers RESUME, SETTINGS, TUTORIAL, and RETURN TO HOMEPAGE. Settings provide music/effects volume and mute, graphics quality, and English/Malay language selection. Low graphics disables scene shadows.

The game saves periodically during active play (every 15 seconds), when pausing, and when returning home. CONTINUE restores the saved checkpoint, rather than necessarily the exact instant the app closed. Saving requires available local storage. Confirming a new run replaces the saved checkpoint; death clears it.

STATS on the home menu shows personal best kill count and survival duration, plus the current saved run. These two best values are tracked independently and can come from different runs. They remain after death or restarting, but clearing app data removes local records and saves.
